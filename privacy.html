This policy applies to the Archangel Messanger Android app and its live service. It does not claim end-to-end encryption.

This policy describes how Archangel Messanger, made by Archangel Vault AI (Michael Manning, Orange NSW, Australia), handles information when you use the Android app and the related live service.

Contact for privacy questions or requests: TopazArchangel@gmail.com.

Who this app is for

The first public listing of Archangel Messanger is for people aged 13 and over. You must be 13 or older to create a new account. We ask for a birth year at sign-up and use it honestly to apply the age rules. We do not invite children under 13, and this app is not part of Google Play’s Families programme.

  • New accounts: 13 or over only.
  • Under 18: no wallet, no purchases, and the account is hidden from search.
  • Adults can choose whether they appear in search, and whether they can be called.

If you are a parent or guardian of a teen, you are responsible for how they use the app.

What we collect

When you use the offline demo only, data stays on your phone. When you sign in to the live service, we collect and store:

  • Account: email address, password (stored by our sign-in provider, not in readable form in the app), @handle, display name, birth year (so we can apply the age rules), and the time the account was created.
  • Profile: bio, profile photo, and privacy choices (who can find you, who can call you, contact requests, read receipts, notification preview).
  • Messages and groups: the content of chats you send, group names and membership, and when messages were sent or read.
  • Photos and files: attachments you choose to send, kept in private storage.
  • Calls: who took part, whether a call was voice or video, when it started and ended, and its state (ringing, accepted, missed, and so on). We store call signalling (the technical offers used to set up the call). We do not keep the live voice or video as a recording on our servers.
  • Safety: people you block, and reports you send.
  • Devices: a push-notification token for the phone, and a simple list of signed-in devices so you can sign out of other devices.

Who helps us run the service

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage, and realtime signalling) on project infrastructure we operate for this app.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), to deliver message and incoming-call notifications to your device. This uses a device push token, and notification text may include a sender or group name depending on your preview setting.
  • Metered Open Relay, used only when a voice or video call cannot connect directly. In that case the relay can see technical connection data such as IP addresses, for the purpose of carrying the call. Call media on the path is protected with DTLS-SRTP in transit. The relay is not used for chat messages.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

How information is protected

The app talks to our service over HTTPS. Voice and video media are protected in transit with DTLS-SRTP. That is not the same as independently reviewed end-to-end encryption, and we do not claim end-to-end encryption. Service operators with the right server credentials could, in principle, access stored messages, files, and call signalling. Do not use this app for information that needs a higher standard of privacy until end-to-end encryption is built and independently reviewed.

How long we keep information

We keep account, chat, file and call-log data until you delete your account, or until we have to keep a limited record to handle abuse, security, or the law. Device push tokens are removed when you sign out or delete the account.

Your choices

  • You can edit your name, bio, photo and privacy switches in the app.
  • You can turn off message-preview text in notifications.
  • You can block or report someone.
  • You can delete your account in the app: open Account, choose Delete account, and confirm with your password. That removes your login and, by design, the profile, messages you sent, memberships, files, devices and related records we hold for that account.
  • If you cannot sign in, email us and we will help with a deletion request. See Delete your account.

International processing

Our providers may store or process data outside Australia. If you use the app, your information may be handled in those locations under this policy and the provider’s terms.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date and keep the policy at the same public address.
