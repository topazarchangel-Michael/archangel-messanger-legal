How to delete your account and data · 17 November 2026 (Australia/Sydney time)

Privacy policy Terms of use Delete your account

Google Play requires a way to request account and data deletion. You can do this in the app today, or follow the steps on this page.

Delete the account in the app

  1. Open Archangel Messanger and sign in.
  2. Go to Account (from the menu, or from Privacy & security → Devices & sign-out).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, type your password, and confirm.

This is the same path Play looks for in the app. It calls our server to delete the signed-in login. Related records (profile, messages you sent, group memberships, files, devices, blocks, reports and call history tied to that account) are removed with it.

If you cannot sign in

Email TopazArchangel@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the subject “Delete Archangel Messanger account”, and include your @handle if you remember it. We will verify that the request is yours and then delete the account. We may need to ask a confirming question so that someone else cannot delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your login, email, @handle, name, bio, photo and birth year.
  • Messages you sent, and files you uploaded.
  • Group memberships, contacts, blocks and reports you made.
  • Call history and device / push-notification tokens.
  • Local account state on the phone after you delete and leave the account screens.

Messages other people sent in a chat with you may still appear on their devices until they delete them. We may also retain a limited record if the law, a safety investigation, or a security issue requires it, and technical backups can take a short time to expire.

The offline demo

If you only used the offline demo and never signed in, you can uninstall the app to remove the sample data on that phone. There is no server account to delete in that case.
